# Open items across the skills

This file collects every open item in `.agents/skills` as of 2026-10-04. An
open item is a question that no sponsor page, IU policy, or IU guidance
answers, or one where those sources disagree. It also lists facts the
2026-10-04 research could not verify, so the skills avoid stating them.

Use it for step 4 of the full review in `MAINTAINING.md`, "Work the open
items." Work the sections in order:

1. **Answerable from a published source.** Search again. Apply each fix to
   the named skill, and cite the source.
2. **Questions for the owning offices.** Send one message per office, with
   every question batched. Close an item only with the date of the reply.

Regenerate this file each quarter with `grep -rn -i "open item"
.agents/skills`. Delete an entry once its skill no longer carries the item.
Entries name skill sections rather than line numbers, which drift.

## Answerable from a published source

Check each again at the next review.

1. **NIH ORCID requirement date.** NIH's investigators page requires a
   linked ORCID iD for everyone completing a Common Form. A secondary source
   dates the requirement to due dates on or after May 25, 2025. No NIH page
   read on 2026-10-04 confirmed that date. `assembling-required-documents`
   states the requirement without a date.
2. **NSF Guidance on Financial Assistance (GFA).** NSF's PAPPG page says the
   GFA "will replace" the PAPPG. A Federal Register notice of 2026-06-24
   took comments until 2026-08-24. No effective date was found.
   `assembling-required-documents`, "Keep this file current."
3. **NIH publication cost cap.** NOT-OD-25-138 asked for comment on capping
   publication costs. No final policy was found as of 2026-10-04, and the
   skills state none.
4. **NSF research security training date.** NSF's page gives December 2,
   2025. Many university notices give October 10, 2025, the first announced
   date. `assembling-required-documents`, "Research security training,"
   uses NSF's date.
5. **IU guidance on the Common Forms and SciENcv.** No IU Research page
   found. IU Libraries keeps a SciENcv guide. `assembling-required-documents`,
   "Keep this file current."

### Office for Research Administration (ORA)

Email: `ora@iu.edu`

**Subject:** Documentation questions: internal deadline, MTDC base, and
submission paths

Hello ORA,

We maintain open documentation that helps IU researchers and their tools
plan proposals. The pages below disagree or are silent. We would like to
cite your answer.

1. Which internal deadline applies? The routing and submission page says
   the KC Proposal Development Document must be "received at ORA by 9 a.m.
   four (4) business days before the funding sponsor's deadline." Policy
   SPA-11-025, last reviewed 2022, sets a day-of-week matrix with later
   times for some components, such as Monday 9 a.m. for NSF technical
   components. From `routing-and-submitting-at-iu/SKILL.md`, "The internal
   deadline."
2. Has IU's rate agreement been amended to cite the $50,000 subaward
   threshold in the 2024 Uniform Guidance? The rates page still includes
   "sub-grants and subcontracts up to the first $25,000." NOT-OD-25-059 lets
   NIH applicants use $50,000 only with "a current NICRA, that cites the
   updated threshold amount." From `budgeting-a-proposal/SKILL.md`, "F&A at
   IU."
3. Which F&A rate should a budget use for years after June 30, 2027, when
   the current rate agreement ends? From `budgeting-a-proposal/SKILL.md`.
4. Does KC submit to Grants.gov system-to-system for all federal sponsors,
   and when does a proposal go through NIH ASSIST instead? The institutional
   information page gives separate details for "Indianapolis NIH proposals
   and Indianapolis PHS proposals using ASSIST."
   From `routing-and-submitting-at-iu/SKILL.md`, "The routing system."
5. Does IU have guidance on letters of institutional commitment, and on
   researchers' use of generative AI when preparing proposals? We found
   neither on research.iu.edu. KB0025830 covers generative AI services in
   general. From `assembling-required-documents/SKILL.md` and
   `planning-a-proposal/SKILL.md`.

Thank you for your help.
