# Assembling the required documents

Verified 2026-10-04 against NIH Grants and Funding pages, NIH Guide notices,
the NSF PAPPG, and IU pages. Sources are listed at the end.

Most returned applications fail on documents, not science. Start the forms
that need other people early: biosketches, Other Support, letters, and
subaward documents. The funding opportunity's own list of required and
optional attachments is the checklist. This skill explains each one.

## NIH forms version

**Required.** NIH applications use FORMS-I, for due dates on or after
January 25, 2025, per the NIH Application Guide and NOT-OD-24-086. Use the
forms package attached to the opportunity.

## Page and length limits

**Required.** From the NIH page limits table, unless the NOFO differs:

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Project Summary/Abstract | 30 lines |
| Project Narrative | 3 sentences |
| Introduction to a resubmission or revision | 1 page |
| Specific Aims | 1 page |
| Research Strategy, R01 and similar | 12 pages |
| Research Strategy, R21, R03, and similar | 6 pages |
| K: Candidate Information and Goals plus Research Strategy | 12 pages combined |
| F: Candidate's Goals 3; Research Training Project Strategy 6; Sponsor Commitment 6 | Pages |
| Letters of support, F and K only | 6 pages |
| Biosketch Common Form plus NIH Supplement | No page limit; SciENcv sets field limits |

## Biosketches and Other Support

**Required.** For NIH due dates on or after January 25, 2026, each senior or
key person uses three forms made in SciENcv: the Biographical Sketch Common
Form and the NIH Biographical Sketch Supplement, certified together as one
PDF for the application, and the Current and Pending (Other) Support Common
Form, usually at Just-in-Time. This is per NOT-OD-26-018. NIH requires
SciENcv "to produce digitally certified PDF(s)."

**Required.** NIH's leniency period ended May 7, 2026. The Common Forms are
required "for all application due dates and Just-In-Time (JIT), Research
Performance Progress Report (RPPR), and Prior Approval submissions on or
after May 8, 2026." From that date, system warnings became errors that stop
submission, per NOT-OD-26-079.

**Required.** The NIH Biosketch Supplement holds a Personal Statement of up
to 3,500 characters with no citations, up to 15 honors, and up to five
Contributions to Science of 2,000 characters each, per NOT-OD-26-018.

**Required.** Each person certifies their own forms, including that they are
"not a party to a malign foreign talent recruitment program," per
NOT-OD-26-018.

**Required.** Each person completing a Common Form must have an ORCID iD
linked to their eRA Commons account, per NIH's page for investigators. Every
PD/PI and senior or key person needs an eRA Commons ID, per NOT-OD-21-109.

**Required.** Report Other Support "for all individuals designated in an
application as senior/key personnel," per IU's Other Support guidance. IU
excludes institutional resources, training awards, prizes, gifts, and
endowed chair funds. Since April 22, 2026, SciENcv's version 2026-1 of the
form accepts zero person months, per NOT-OD-26-079.

**Recommended.** Give every key person a date to finish and certify in
SciENcv, two weeks before the internal deadline. A person may delegate
editing in SciENcv, but must certify their own forms.

NIH usually asks for Other Support at Just-in-Time, not at application. See
"Just-in-Time" below.

### NSF

**Required.** NSF requires the Biographical Sketch and Current and Pending
(Other) Support Common Forms through SciENcv for each senior or key person,
per PAPPG 24-1. The biosketch has "no page or character limit" except where
noted.

**Required.** NSF also needs, per PAPPG 24-1 Chapter II:

- Collaborators and Other Affiliations, on NSF's template, for each senior
  or key person.
- Synergistic Activities, "up to one-page," with up to five examples.
- A Mentoring Plan of "no more than one page" when funding postdocs or
  graduate students.

## Research security training

**Required.** For NIH due dates on or after May 25, 2026, each senior or key
person certifies completing research security training "within 12 months of
the date of application submission," in their SciENcv biosketch, per
NOT-OD-26-017.

**Required.** NSF requires the same certification within 12 months before
submission, effective December 2, 2025, per NSF Important Notice 149.

**Required.** IU requires senior and key personnel on federal proposals to
complete research security training "in the twelve (12) months prior to
submission of a funding proposal and annually thereafter," per IU policy
RP-11-012.

**Open item.** NSF's page gives December 2, 2025 as the training effective
date. Many university notices give October 10, 2025, the date first
announced. Use NSF's date.

## Facilities and Other Resources, and Equipment

**Required.** NIH and NSF each ask for a description of facilities and
resources. NSF uses it "to assess the adequacy of the resources" for both
review criteria, and forbids quantifiable financial information in it, per
PAPPG 24-1.

Under NIH's Simplified Review Framework, Factor 3, Expertise and Resources,
covers the investigators and the institutional environment. This attachment
and the biosketches carry that evidence. See `writing-the-research-strategy`.

Sources of IU text:

- IU Research offers a "Brief of IU for Facilities and Equipment Template"
  on its proposal development page.
- UITS prepared a cyberinfrastructure facilities statement, per KB0024649.
  It is in Google at IU and needs an IU login. Delete parts that do not
  apply. The research-technologies repository covers IU computing.
- Indiana CTSI provides facilities text for its services through its
  letter-of-support page.
- Core facilities provide their own descriptions. See the research-cores
  repository.

**Recommended.** Describe only what this project will use, and how. A
generic institutional page earns no credit.

## Letters of support and collaboration

**Required.** NIH letters go in one combined PDF in the Letters of Support
field, not the Appendix. They must not contain "data / figures / tables /
graphs, preliminary data, methods," and applications with such letters "may be withdrawn from the
review process," per the FORMS-I Research Plan instructions.

**Required.** NSF accepts letters of collaboration "limited to stating the
intent to collaborate," with no endorsement or evaluation. Letters of
support are not allowed unless the solicitation asks for them, per PAPPG
24-1 Chapter II.

**Recommended.** Ask for a letter from anyone who commits something the
proposal depends on: a collaborator's time, data access, samples, a core's
capacity, or a site's participation. Send each writer a draft that states
the commitment and a deadline. The writer edits it and signs it on their
own letterhead.

**Recommended.** For data held by someone else, such as a biobank or a
health system, the letter should state the access path and its approval
steps. It should not state results.

## Data Management and Sharing Plan

**Required.** NIH's DMS Policy applies to research that generates scientific
data, per NOT-OD-21-013. For due dates on or after May 25, 2026, applicants
use the new structured format, per NOT-OD-26-046. It has yes or no sharing
questions, a limitations explanation of "300 words maximum," and a table of
data types and repositories.

**Required.** NIH program staff assess the plan. "Peer reviewers are not
asked to comment on the DMS Plan," per NIH's writing a DMS plan page.

**Required.** Plans submitted in the old format must be updated at JIT or in
the next progress report, per NOT-OD-26-100. Changing an approved plan no
longer needs prior approval. From October 1, 2026, report changes in the
RPPR instead, per the same notice.

**Required.** NSF limits its Data Management and Sharing Plan to two pages,
created in the Research.gov tool, per NSF 26-202.

For the content of a plan, use the research-data repository's
`planning-data-management-and-sharing` skill. IU Research publishes
element-by-element guidance for the NIH format, and the Research Data
Commons offers free consultations.

## Human subjects and clinical trials

**Required.** An NIH application with human subjects completes the PHS Human
Subjects and Clinical Trials Information form, per the Application Guide.

**Required.** A study is a clinical trial under NIH's definition if all four
answers are yes: it involves human participants, they are prospectively
assigned to an intervention, the study evaluates the intervention's effect,
and the effect is on a health-related biomedical or behavioral outcome, per
NIH's clinical trial definition page.

**Required.** For due dates on or after May 25, 2026, Basic Experimental
Studies with Humans (BESH) are no longer clinical trials, per NOT-OD-26-032.
Human subjects protections and the DMS Policy still apply.

**Required.** Apply to a NOFO that allows the study's type. A clinical trial
needs a NOFO that says clinical trials are required or optional.

**Required.** Multi-site non-exempt human subjects research uses a single
IRB, per NOT-OD-16-094 and 45 CFR 46.114. NIH asks for the single IRB of
record at JIT, not at application. At IU, contact the HRPP's single IRB
office "as soon as possible prior to submitting your grant proposal," per
IU's single IRB guide.

**Required.** Inclusion of women, minorities, and individuals across the
lifespan remains NIH policy, per NIH's inclusion page. Reviewers judge
inclusion plans under Factor 2, Rigor and Feasibility, per NOT-OD-24-010.

**Required.** NIH no longer requires or evaluates a Plan for Enhancing
Diverse Perspectives (PEDP), per NIH's PEDP page.

**Recommended.** Secondary use of existing data, such as biobank or EHR
data, is still human subjects research unless the IRB determines otherwise.
Ask the HRPP for a determination or exemption before writing the section.
IU uses Kuali Protocols for IRB submissions.

| Attachment | Rule | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authentication of Key Biological and/or Chemical Resources | Separate attachment; not scored | NIH rigor guidance |
| Appendix | Only blank data-collection forms, simple interview question lists, and blank consent forms, unless the NOFO allows more | NOT-OD-17-098 |
| Multiple PI Leadership Plan | Required when there is more than one PD/PI | NIH Application Guide |
| Vertebrate Animals | Required when animals are used | NIH Application Guide |
| Resource sharing plans | As the NOFO requires, including Genomic Data Sharing | NIH Application Guide |

Each row states a rule from the NIH notice or guide in its last column.

## Just-in-Time

**Required.** NIH asks for Just-in-Time information when an application may
be funded. "This request is not an indicator of an award decision," per
NIH's JIT page. It usually covers:

- updated Other Support on the Common Form;
- IRB and IACUC approval dates;
- human subjects education certification;
- the single IRB of record;
- an updated DMS Plan, where NOT-OD-26-100 applies.

**Required.** "Submission of pending (e.g., conditional, pre-approval,
interim) or expired IRB approval dates is not acceptable," per NOT-OD-26-043.

**Required.** At IU, ORA submits JIT. It needs "at least four business days"
for review, per RAS-001. See `routing-and-submitting-at-iu`.

## Keep this file current

- Re-read the NIH Common Forms pages and SciENcv each review. Field limits
  change in SciENcv before the notices catch up.
- Watch for FORMS-J.
- Re-read the NSF PAPPG page for its replacement, the Guidance on Financial
  Assistance, which had no effective date as of 2026-10-04.
- **Open item.** IU Research has no page on the Common Forms or SciENcv as of
  2026-10-04. IU Libraries keeps a SciENcv guide.
