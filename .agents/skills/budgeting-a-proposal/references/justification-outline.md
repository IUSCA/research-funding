# Budget justification outline

Verified 2026-10-04. Read the main `SKILL.md` first. This outline follows the
order of the NIH R&R budget sections, A through H. The NOFO, the NIH Application
Guide, or the NSF PAPPG may require another order or a page limit. Follow
them first.

Every line answers two questions: what does it buy, and which aim needs it.
Leave out salaries in a shared copy. Keep the filled justification with the
proposal.

```markdown
# Budget justification

## A. Senior and key personnel
<Name>, <role>, <N> calendar/academic/summer months in each year.
<One or two sentences: responsibilities, and the aims they work on.>
<Note any year where effort changes, and why.>

## B. Other personnel
<Role, such as postdoctoral researcher or data analyst>, <N> months.
<Responsibilities and aims.> <Whether the person is named or to be hired.>

## Fringe benefits
Calculated at IU's rates for each employee group, as published by IU
Research for the budget period.

## C. Equipment
<Item, cost, and why existing equipment cannot do the work.>

## D. Travel
<Each trip: purpose, destination type, travelers, and cost basis.>

## E. Participant or trainee support costs
<NSF and some NIH programs only. Stipends, travel, and fees for participants.>

## F. Other direct costs
1. Materials and supplies: <categories and basis>.
2. Publication costs: <basis>.
3. Consultant services: <who, what, rate, and days>.
4. Computer services: <service, rate, and units>.
5. Subawards: <site, scope, and aims; the site's own justification follows>.
6. Equipment or facility rental/user fees: <core, service, rate, units, and
   aim>. Ask your grant administrator whether IU puts core charges here or
   under Other.
7. Alterations and renovations: <only if the NOFO allows them>.
8. Other: <each item and basis, such as data management and sharing costs:
   curation effort, repository fees, storage>.

## H. Indirect costs (F&A)
IU's federally negotiated rate of <rate> for <activity>, <on or off campus>,
on the MTDC base, per IU's rate agreement.

## Cost sharing (only if required)
<What is committed, by whom, and the approval on the routing record.>
```

Notes:

- Effort in months must match the person months on the budget form and in
  each person's Other Support.
- A core's rate comes from the core's own published rate sheet or quote.
  The research-cores repository covers IU core facilities.
- For an NIH modular budget, write only the personnel justification and any
  consortium and additional narrative the Application Guide asks for.
