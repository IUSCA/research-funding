# Budgeting a proposal

Verified 2026-10-04 against IU Research, NIH, NSF, and federal regulation
pages. Sources are listed at the end.

A budget is a promise about the work. Reviewers check that it matches the
aims, and the sponsor holds you to it. Build it with your grant administrator
or RDS Pre-Award Services, never alone. See `routing-and-submitting-at-iu`.

## Start from the work

**Recommended.** Write the budget from the Approach, aim by aim. For each
task, name who does it, how much of their time it takes, and what it costs
besides people. Then total by category. A budget built from last year's
spreadsheet drifts from the aims.

## Personnel and person months

Effort is stated in person months: the share of an appointment, times its
length.

| Appointment | 10 percent effort is |
| --- | --- |
| 12-month (calendar) | 1.2 calendar months |
| 9-month (academic) | 0.9 academic months |
| 3-month summer | 0.3 summer months |

- **Required.** "'Zero percent' effort or 'as needed' is not an acceptable
  level of involvement for Key Personnel," per IU's RAS-005.
- **Required.** A later reduction of 25 percent or more in a key person's
  effort needs sponsor approval, per RAS-005. Budget realistic effort now.
- **Required.** Total committed effort across all awards cannot exceed 100
  percent of a person's appointment, per the NIH Grants Policy Statement on
  commitment of effort. Other Support shows every commitment;
  see `assembling-required-documents`.

### NIH salary cap

**Required.** NIH limits the salary rate it pays to Executive Level II of the
federal pay scale. The cap is $228,000 effective January 1, 2026, per
NOT-OD-26-034 as corrected by NOT-OD-26-038. Request salary at the capped
rate times effort. Salary above the cap is not charged to the award. Check
for the new notice each January.

**Observed 2026-10-04 on the IU Research rates page.** IU fringe rates
effective July 1, 2026:

| Group | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| A, academic exempt | 33.14% |
| B, summer salary for 10-month appointees | 24.32% |
| C, professional exempt and non-exempt | 34.42% |
| D, non-exempt staff | 37.29% |
| E, hourly non-student | 19.28% |
| H, graduate student health insurance | $1,995 for Fall 2026; $2,764 for Spring 2027 |

Summer months for academic-year faculty use Group B, not Group A. Fringe
rates change each July. Re-check the page for each budget year.

**Recommended.** For any estimate, read the IU Research rates page at the
time. Use this table only to check the result. Core facility charges come
from a current quote, never from this repository; see
`budgeting-core-services-in-proposals` in research-cores.

## F&A at IU

**Required.** IU charges F&A at its federally negotiated rates, on a
modified total direct cost (MTDC) base, per its rate agreement with HHS.
IU policy SPA-11-007 says budgets "should include [F&A] to the greatest
extent possible."

**Observed 2026-10-04 on the IU Research rates page.** For the agreement
period July 1, 2019 to June 30, 2027:

| Activity | On campus | Off campus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organized research | 58.5% | 26% |
| Instruction | 46.5% | 26% |
| Other sponsored activities | 32% | 26% |

The page publishes one rate for all of IU, with no split between
Bloomington and Indianapolis.

**Required.** IU's MTDC base includes "sub-grants and subcontracts up to the
first $25,000 of each," per the IU Research rates page. The 2024 Uniform
Guidance raised the federal threshold to $50,000, per 2 CFR 200.1. NIH lets
an applicant use $50,000 only with "a current NICRA, that cites the updated
threshold amount," per NOT-OD-25-059. IU's does not, as of 2026-10-04. Use
$25,000, the budget script's default, unless your grant administrator says
IU's agreement has changed.

**Required.** MTDC excludes equipment, capital expenditures, patient care,
rental of off-site facilities, tuition remission, scholarships and
fellowships, participant support costs, and each subaward above the
threshold, per 2 CFR 200.1.

**Required.** An F&A waiver for a federal sponsor needs approval of the
Associate Vice President for Research Administration, per SPA-11-007.

**Open item.** The IU rate agreement ends June 30, 2027. A budget whose
years run past that date will use rates not yet negotiated. Ask ORA which
rate to budget for out-years.

### The 2025 federal F&A caps

In 2025 NIH and NSF each announced a 15 percent cap on F&A for institutions
of higher education. Courts blocked both.

- **NSF.** The NSF policy (NSF 25-034) was vacated on June 20, 2025, in
  AAU v. NSF. The government moved to dismiss its appeal on September 26, 2025. NSF
  says "Effective May 19, 2025, NSF is issuing awards using the
  institution's applicable federally negotiated indirect cost rate."
- **NIH.** The NIH policy (NOT-OD-25-068) was permanently enjoined. The
  First Circuit affirmed on January 5, 2026, and the government did not seek
  Supreme Court review, per a FABBS report of April 23, 2026. **External.**
  The President's FY2027 budget request again includes the 15 percent cap,
  per the same report. Congress had not adopted it as of 2026-10-04.

**Recommended.** Budget at IU's negotiated rate unless the NOFO or a current
sponsor notice says otherwise.

## NIH budget forms

- **Required.** Use a modular budget when direct costs, less consortium
  F&A, are $250,000 or less in every year, in $25,000 modules, for the
  activity codes and applicants that allow it, per the NIH Application
  Guide. Otherwise use the detailed R&R budget.
- NIH no longer requires applicants requesting $500,000 or more in direct
  costs in a year to contact the institute first, per NOT-OD-26-019 of
  December 3, 2025. The acceptance letter is gone too. **Recommended.**
  Still talk to the program officer about a large budget. Many institutional
  guides, and older agents, still state the old rule.
- **Required.** NIH does not allow a modular budget for foreign
  institutions or small business applications, per NIH's budget page.
- **Required.** New NIH awards may not include foreign subawards, per
  NOT-OD-25-104. International collaborations apply through the PF5 or UF5
  multi-component structure, per NOT-OD-25-155.
- **Required.** A modular budget still needs a personnel justification and
  a consortium justification, per the Application Guide.

## Lines that need care

| Line | Care needed | Source or skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core facility and service center charges | Use a written quote at the core's internal rate. Core charges are services, so they are in the MTDC base. Which NIH budget line IU uses for them is an open item there. | research-cores `budgeting-core-services-in-proposals` |
| Computing and storage | Most IU research computing is free at base levels; some storage is billed. | research-technologies `planning-research-computing-work` |
| Data management and sharing | Curation, repository fees, and storage are allowable costs on NIH and NSF awards. | research-data `planning-data-management-and-sharing` |
| Subawards | Each site sends a budget, justification, and commitment form from its sponsored-programs office. | IU proposal tools page; `assembling-required-documents` |
| Equipment | Items at or above the equipment threshold are outside MTDC. IU's threshold is in its policy; the federal ceiling is $10,000, per 2 CFR 200.1. | Ask your grant administrator |
| Participant support (NSF) | Stipends, travel, and fees for participants, not employees. Outside MTDC. | NSF PAPPG |
| Patient care | Research-only clinical costs. Outside MTDC. | 2 CFR 200.1 |
| Data access fees | Biobank, EHR extract, or analyst fees. Many data holders recover analyst time. | research-data `accessing-health-and-clinical-data` |
| Travel | Name trips and their purpose. | Sponsor guide |

## Budget justification

**Recommended.** For each line, say what it buys and why the aims need it.
Name each person's role and the aims they work on. Explain any year that
jumps. Reviewers flag effort that does not match the work.

**Required.** NSF limits the budget justification to five pages per
proposal, and per subaward, per the PAPPG.

## Cost sharing

**Required.** Cost share "must be approved by the appropriate University
offices… at the time the proposal is routed" and documented on the routing
form and in the budget, per RAS-011.

**Recommended.** Offer cost sharing only when the opportunity requires it.
Committed cost sharing must be tracked and reported for the life of the
award.

## Foundations

**Required.** Foundations set their own indirect cost limits in each call.
For example, the Alzheimer's Association caps indirect costs "at 10 percent
of total direct costs" for one program. **External.** The Gates Foundation's
published policy allows "up to a 10% indirect cost rate" for U.S.
universities; the policy document is dated 2017.

## Draft it with the script

[scripts/budget-calc.py](scripts/budget-calc.py) drafts a multi-year budget
from a JSON file of people, effort, and costs. Every rate is an input.

```bash
s=.agents/skills/budgeting-a-proposal/scripts/budget-calc.py
$s --example > budget.json     # edit rates, people, and lines
$s budget.json                 # Markdown tables and NIH checks
```

It applies a salary cap to annualized salary, computes fringe, the MTDC base,
and F&A, and checks the NIH modular threshold. It flags years of $500,000
or more, which NIH no longer requires you to clear in advance. Its output is a
draft for your grant administrator. Keep the filled JSON with the proposal,
not in this repository, because it holds salaries.

[references/justification-outline.md](references/justification-outline.md)
gives an outline for the budget justification.

## Keep this file current

- Re-read the IU rates page each July, when fringe rates change.
- Re-read the NIH salary cap notice each January. Update the cap above.
- Watch the rate agreement renewal, due by June 30, 2027.
- Watch the NIH and NSF F&A cap litigation and any new F&A policy.
