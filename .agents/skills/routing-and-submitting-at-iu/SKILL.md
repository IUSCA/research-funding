# Routing and submitting a proposal at IU

Verified 2026-10-04 against IU Research pages, IU policies, and the Research
Administration Standards. Sources are listed at the end.

IU submits proposals, not individual researchers. Every proposal goes
through the Office for Research Administration (ORA). Start routing early,
because IU's internal deadline comes days before the sponsor's.

**Required.** "Proposals and post-proposal actions may not be submitted
directly to the funding source without the prior approval of ORA," per IU
policy SPA-11-025.

## The internal deadline

**Required.** "The KC Proposal Development document must be routed for
internal approvals and received at ORA by 9 a.m. four (4) business days
before the funding sponsor's deadline," per the IU Research routing and
submission page. It adds: "Allow additional time for complex proposals such
as collaborations, cost-share, or subawards."

**Open item.** IU policy SPA-11-025, last reviewed 2022, sets a day-of-week
matrix instead. For a Monday sponsor deadline it asks for federal
administrative components the previous Wednesday, and technical components
as late as Monday 9 a.m. for NSF. The routing page's flat four business days
is stricter. **Recommended.** Plan to the four-business-day rule, and ask
your grant administrator if a later technical upload is possible.

**Required.** "Proposals submitted after the internal deadlines stated above
are at risk of not being submitted," per SPA-11-025. A late proposal that ORA
accepts gets "a compliance review only."

**Required.** For actions outside KC, such as Just-in-Time, pre-proposals,
and rebudgets, "at least four business days is needed for administrative
review and approval by ORA," per RAS-001.

`planning-a-proposal` has a script that computes these dates. Pass IU
holidays and closures to it.

## Who helps, in order

1. **Your unit's grant administrator.** "If your unit has a grant
   administrator, you will work with them," per the routing page. They build
   the KC document and budget with you.
2. **Research Development Services (RDS) Pre-Award Services**, if your unit
   has none. Submit the RDS request form; "a Pre-Award Specialist will be
   assigned." RDS asks for requests "at least 4 weeks before the due date."
3. **ORA.** "After the KC Proposal Development Document is received by
   [ORA], a Grants Services Administrator will be assigned as your point of
   contact for submission," per the routing page.

If you do not know your contact, email ora@iu.edu. See
`getting-help-with-proposals` for the rest of IU's proposal support.

## The routing system

**Observed 2026-10-04 on research.iu.edu.** IU routes proposals in Kuali
Coeus (KC), reached through One.IU as "Kuali Coeus (KC) Grants." The routing
record is the "KC Proposal Development Document." The Kuali Research Systems
page lists KC as on-premises and says "Kuali Classic Systems will be
upgraded to NextGen in the coming years."

ORA's training page has a "Kuali Coeus Tutorial: Proposal Development
Document (PDD) Creation."

**Required.** The approvers are "the submitting unit and all collaborating
units, the dean or administrative unit head for each, and ORA Proposal &
Award Services," per RAS-001. RAS-001 superseded SPA-11-018 on August 28,
2026.

**Open item.** Whether KC submits to Grants.gov system-to-system for every
sponsor is not stated on current pages. IU's institutional information page
gives separate applicant details for "Indianapolis NIH proposals and
Indianapolis PHS proposals using ASSIST," so some NIH applications go
through ASSIST. Ask your Grants Services Administrator which
path your proposal takes.

## Checks IU makes before submission

| Check | Rule | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PI eligibility | Full-time academic appointees or staff qualify "as determined by the dean or director." Adjuncts, visitors, and people on unpaid leave need dean approval and a PI/PD Eligibility Form to ORA. | SPA-11-006 |
| Cost sharing | Cost share "must be approved by the appropriate University offices… at the time the proposal is routed" and "documented on the routing form and in the proposal budget." | RAS-011 |
| F&A waiver | Federal waivers need approval of the Associate Vice President for Research Administration. Others may be approved by the dean or RC head. | SPA-11-007 |
| Key personnel effort | "'Zero percent' effort or 'as needed' is not an acceptable level of involvement for Key Personnel." | RAS-005 |
| Research security training | Senior and key personnel on federal proposals complete training "in the twelve (12) months prior to submission of a funding proposal and annually thereafter." | RP-11-012 |
| Foreign engagements | Other Support and biosketch disclosures must be complete. Give IU copies of foreign agreements. | RP-11-012 |
| Export control | Contact the Research Security Office "before engaging in a transaction that may involve export control concerns." | RP-11-012, RP-11-009 |
| Conflict of interest | Disclose in Kuali Conflict of Interest annually and "within 30 days of a change in interest." | IU COI disclosure page |

Each row above is **Required** by the IU policy or standard named in its
last column.

**Open item.** The COI page states no proposal-time trigger. NIH's financial
conflict of interest rule, 42 CFR 50 Subpart F, requires disclosure of
significant financial interests "no later than the time of application" for
investigators planning to take part in PHS-funded research. Ask coi@iu.edu
how IU applies that at routing.

## Institutional facts the forms need

Your grant administrator fills these in. They are listed so an agent does
not guess them.

- IU's UEIs and authorizing official are on the IU Research institutional
  information page. Use that page, not memory, because IU has more than one
  entity.
- F&A and fringe rates are on the IU Research rates page. See
  `budgeting-a-proposal`.

## Limited submissions

**Required.** When a sponsor limits how many proposals an institution may
send, IU picks them through an internal competition. "InfoReady lists IU's
current limited submission opportunities," per the IU Research limited
submissions page. Contact limsub@iu.edu. See `finding-funding-opportunities`.

## Foundations and corporations

**Required.** "Some organizations have requested coordination through the
business partnerships and foundation relations teams, including those on the
critical contact list," per IU Research's corporate and foundation page.
Talk to Corporate and Foundation Relations before approaching a foundation
on that list. The list needs an IU login. CFR "can review your proposal
before it is routed," per the routing page.

## After submission

- **NIH.** **Practice.** Check the assembled application image in eRA
  Commons as soon as ORA submits, and tell your Grants Services
  Administrator of any problem the same day. Watch for the review assignment and summary statement there.
  `responding-to-reviews-and-resubmitting` covers what comes back.
- **NSF.** Status is in Research.gov.
- **Just-in-Time.** When NIH asks for JIT, ORA submits it. Updated Other
  Support, IRB approval, and human subjects education go through ORA, which
  needs four business days, per RAS-001. See
  `assembling-required-documents`.

**Practice.** Errors found after ORA submits are fixed by ORA, not by you.
Send them to your Grants Services Administrator at once. NIH's correction
window closes at the sponsor deadline.

## Keep this file current

- Re-read the routing page and SPA-11-025 each review. The open item on the
  deadline conflict closes only with ORA's answer or a revised policy.
- The Research Administration Standards are new as of August 28, 2026. Watch
  for more SPA policies moving to RAS numbers.
- Watch for the KC NextGen upgrade. It will change the system name and
  steps.
