# Getting help with proposals

Verified 2026-10-04 against the IU Research, Indiana CTSI, and IU School of
Medicine pages named in Sources.

Ask early. Most IU proposal services ask for weeks of lead time, and none can
help in the last four business days. Send each question to the office that
decides it.

## Writing guides and templates

IU Research publishes guides and templates on its proposal development page.
They include NIH and NSF funding guides, an NIH Specific Aims guide, an NSF
Broader Impacts guide, and templates for the NIH R01, K, K99/R00, and R35,
and NSF and NSF CAREER. It also offers a "Brief of IU for Facilities and
Equipment Template" and internal budget templates. Some items need an IU
login.

## Talk to the program officer

**Recommended.** Email the program officer named in the funding opportunity
with a one-page summary or draft aims. Ask whether the project fits the
program, which institute or division would take it, and whether timing
matters. NIH and NSF both list program contacts in each opportunity.

**Practice.** A short, specific email with an aims page gets an answer more
often than a request to "discuss my idea."

## What to send

**Recommended.** Put these in the first message to any proposal office.
They save a round of questions:

- The sponsor, the funding opportunity number, and its URL.
- The sponsor deadline, and whether it is a resubmission.
- The PI, co-investigators, and any other institutions involved.
- A rough budget and project period, if known.
- Whether there are human subjects, animals, clinical trials, foreign
  components, cost sharing, or subawards.
- What you want from this office, and by when.

Do not send participant data or unpublished results that the office does not
need. Describe them by type and size.

## Keep this file current

- Check each contact and page each review. Offices at IU are renamed often.
- **Open item.** No lead time is published for CTSI Project Development
  Teams, CTSI letters of support, or IUSM research support. Ask each office
  and record the answer here.
- **Open item.** The current status of the IUSM grant-writing program found
  only in search results was not confirmed. Do not list it until an IUSM
  page names it.
